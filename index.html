01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGE

A SHORT INTRODUCTION

A little bit of business.
A little bit of making.
A lot still to explore.

I have always been drawn to the practical side of ideas: what makes someone notice a product, trust a brand, try something new, or come back again. Studying business gives me a way to understand those questions; creating things gives me a way to test them.

Growing up in Nagpur and spending time around my family's retail business has made business feel less like a chapter in a textbook and more like something happening every day — in conversations, choices, presentation and small details.

I'm not presenting this as a finished journey. I'm documenting what I'm learning, what I'm trying, and what I want to get better at.

01

RETAIL / CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

A shop floor is a classroom.

Retail is full of small signals: the question a customer asks, the product they pick up twice, the price they pause at, or the detail that makes them feel comfortable. Paying attention to these moments is a useful way to understand people.

↗
02

CONTENT / ATTENTION

The first few seconds are a promise.

A hook can earn attention, but it cannot carry the whole story. The rest of the content has to deliver on that opening. I'm interested in finding the balance between curiosity, clarity and a reason to keep watching.

↗
03

LEARNING / SPORT

Consistency is less dramatic than motivation.

Sport is a reminder that improvement often looks repetitive from the outside. The useful part is turning up, paying attention to technique, taking feedback and doing the next session a little better.
